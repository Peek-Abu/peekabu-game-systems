# Hub signs and boards (owner 11-12)

Build kit mock round 2 (#1 station toppers, #2 number-and-picture boards) and ideas #5 (coloured lanes) and #6 (outline on
look). Gates: [2026-11-12-hub-signs-gates.md](2026-11-12-hub-signs-gates.md).

## Decisions

- **Toppers sit on Studio anchors.** A thin part tagged `DepotTopper` with attribute `Topper = <station>` marks where a
  topper stands and which way it faces (its LookVector faces the readers). The client dresses it (plate, icon, word,
  backing, light pool, lane). The owner moves an anchor to move everything. The time clock and the daily sheet are
  built by the server, so their toppers stand on top of their sign shells (derived on the client from the shell part).
- **Lockers keep their GLOOM / GLEAM toppers** (Studio art from the round that inspired the idea); no second topper.
- **Lanes** start `LaneStart` studs in front of the anchor (attribute, default 2) and run `LaneLength` studs (default 6)
  along its facing, on the floor found by a downward ray. Station colour, flat, with chevrons, never glowing (the gold
  training trail stays the one glowing path).
- **Outline on look** listens to `ShiftPromptStore.shown` (the prompt already picks what the camera aims at). Hit-box
  parts (fully transparent) outline their parent model instead.
- **Rank board in the world:** a new tagged SurfaceGui shell on the rank poster's face; the old Studio gui is disabled,
  not deleted. The close-up scene (`depot.RankChart`) keeps the names.
- New icons: radio, bell (round-2 renders), coffee (the hub's mug, rendered 11-12). Others reuse UIAssets toon icons.
