# Odd Hours: the launch sound mix (2026-11-10)

The final mix pass for launch (Launch List). Mixing only: no sound was generated or replaced.

## Where the mix lives
- **Volume groups** (`SoundConstants.DEFAULT_GROUP_VOLUMES`, the player's two sliders): Music 0.5, SFX 1, UI 0.8,
  Ambient 0.7. Unchanged.
- **Mix buses** (`SoundConstants.BUSES`, new): one SoundGroup per category, nested in its volume group, so a whole
  category is one dB number and the sliders still cover it. A sound joins one with `bus` on its `SoundDef`
  (`SoundRegistry` checks the bus exists and sits in the sound's group). All trims start at 0 dB; tune here.

  | Bus | Group | What |
  |---|---|---|
  | Threat | SFX | Mr. Harlow in the world: tell, typing, creaks, crack, ambush, sit, swivel, voice, grab, power-down, and your heartbeat |
  | Stings | SFX | 2D story stings: spotted, chase and catch stings, fired, rehired, tier and overtime stings, the clock-out horn, training's slam and relief |
  | Reward | SFX | loot reveals, appraisals, jackpot, glint, banking (swoosh, cha-ching), punch-in, contract met, results jingles |
  | Action | SFX | the crew's hands: vacuum, cargo, abilities, hiding, emotes, training's door creak |
  | Steps | SFX | every footstep, surface layer, jump, land and body fall |
  | Beds | Ambient | the office and yard beds |
  | Spots | Ambient | positional ambience: sweeteners, phone, lift, lamp, vending, radio, road, TV, typing |

  No bus (straight into the group): Music (hub, dread, chase stems, overtime ticking), UI, and three world SFX
  (light zap, car tick, auto door).
- **Per-sound volume** (each feature's `Data/*Assets`): matches a file's loudness to its band.
- **Scattered gains that stay where they are** (they are dynamic, driven by game state):
  `OccupantConstants.DREAD.CHASE_MUSIC_GAIN` (2.5, +8 dB on the chase stems), `HEART_GAIN` (0.75 x the dread's heart
  share), the dread duck/muffle (`DREAD.LOOKS`), the results duck (`ShiftConstants.RESULTS_DUCK_*`).

## How it was measured
Every registered sound (202) was played in a Studio Play client through `AudioPlayer -> AudioAnalyzer` with no output
wired (silent), 2026-11-10, Shift place. Per sound: the loudest 0.4 s RMS window ("mom", dBFS, unweighted), the RMS
over active frames, and the peak. The effective level E = mom + 20 log10(volume) + group dB (Music -6.0, UI -1.9,
Ambient -3.1). Against offline LUFS of the masters we have, mom reads about 2.5 dB under momentary LUFS.
(The Open Cloud key cannot download audio, so the in-Studio route is the only one that covers every uploaded asset.)

## 3D roll-off
Unchanged: every positional sound keeps its own `rollOffMaxDistance` / `rollOffMinDistance` (owner rule: things play from
where they are). Harlow's cues reach 55-90 studs (through walls), the crew's actions 20-70, the yard's far sources
use large mins (owls 25/220, road 40/360).
