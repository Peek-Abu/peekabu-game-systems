# Odd Hours play-check route (2026-11-05)

Two Studio sessions that cover every "waiting for your check" row on the Odd Hours Launch List
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Ax27MKsdzqr2gkX1b9dESB). The live copy is the page's Play-check route tab (db
collection `steps`). Tick a row on the list only after it works in real play.

## Before you start

### S0. Save first

- **Do:** In Studio, SAVE both places (Odd Hours and Odd Hours: Shift). Set the Workspace attribute RunTests to false.
- **Good:** Both places saved. The new truck, yard props and Cappy's shorts are kept.

## Session 1: on your own

### S1. Hub: your stuff

- **Do:** Join the hub. Open the Inventory. Look at the crew cards, a character page, the stat lines, the lanyard, the Vacuums tab. Punch the time clock. Look at the INVITE FRIENDS / JOIN GROUP row.
- **Good:** Each stat has one short line ("Run faster."). The employee waves or reacts when picked. The time clock stamps your card. Nothing is cut off on screen.
- **Checks:** Pick your employee and spawn as them; Inventory and character pages; Idles, fidgets, menu reactions, free emotes; Vacuum skins and the Vacuums tab; Rank lanyard and badge; Timecard and Daily Work Order; Friend 2x XP, referral, group Gleam, favorite ask

### S2. Shop and Lockers

- **Do:** Open the Shop: Featured, Skins, Gleam, Passes. Open a product page and say No. Open a Lost & Found Locker (use an admin grant if you need keys).
- **Good:** Prices and words fit. The Locker shows its odds, plays its opening, and the reveal screen pops by rarity.
- **Checks:** Reveal screen by rarity; Hire page fits on every screen; Hire reveals; Shop screens; Lost & Found Lockers; Passes tab with Big Crew

### S3. Wear a Legendary skin

- **Do:** Put on Bubble Buddy (then PJ Cappy, then Sheriff Prickles). Stand still for 20 seconds, then walk. Try one Gloom-Touched skin too.
- **Good:** Standing still plays the skin's own idle with its prop, and sometimes a fidget. The prop goes away as soon as you move.
- **Checks:** Legendary skins have their own idles; The three Legendary skins; Gloom-Touched skins

### S4. Trucks and stickers

- **Do:** Walk to the truck bays. Step onto a bay to host. Open the chat wheel: send a ping, then throw a sticker and jump right away.
- **Good:** Empty bays show a see-through ghost truck; your truck pops in. It is the big Moving Van with "WE CLEAN UP THE NIGHT!". The sticker is thrown with one arm and stays where it landed when you jump.
- **Checks:** Truck v3 Moving Van, bigger bed; Legendary sticker packs; Chat wheel, pings and stickers; Sticker toss

### S5. The yard: look

- **Do:** Start a solo shift. In the yard, walk from the truck to the dock door and look around.
- **Good:** Bins, pallets, cones, vending machine, benches and cars look like they belong. Moths fly round the lamps, fireflies in the grass. A lamp post on the walk may lean (it is an anomaly).
- **Checks:** Yard set dressing; Cars in the car park; Moths and fireflies; Leaning lamp post anomaly

### S6. The yard: listen

- **Do:** Stand still near a lamp, then near the guard booth. Walk on asphalt, grass and a puddle.
- **Good:** Wind, far cars and crickets; lamps buzz; the booth radio plays. Each ground sounds different under your feet.
- **Checks:** Yard sounds from their sources; Outdoor footsteps

### S7. Go inside and back out

- **Do:** Go through the dock door. Then come back out while Mr. Harlow walks around inside.
- **Good:** Inside, the yard sounds are gone. Outside, you hear none of the office: no Harlow footsteps, no phones.
- **Checks:** Inside sounds are silent outside

### S8. The truck up close

- **Do:** Walk up the ramp, throw an item into the bed, use the load prompt, look around inside the box.
- **Good:** The camera stays inside the box and never goes through the walls. The time clock and overtime sheet are easy to reach.
- **Checks:** Truck v3 Moving Van, bigger bed

### S9. Quit early

- **Do:** Start a new shift. Clock out right away without cleaning anything.
- **Good:** The results card shows no XP and no pay for the shift.
- **Checks:** Clock out at once pays nothing

### S10. Ability

- **Do:** Play a shift. Use your employee's ability (Let's Go!, Spot It! or Potted Plant).
- **Good:** It plays its sound and effect. Let's Go! leaves no speed trail.
- **Checks:** Abilities: Let's Go!, Spot It!, Potted Plant; Character sounds

### S11. Stats

- **Do:** Type employeestatset me all 1 and sprint down a long hall. Then all 5 and do it again. Carry the biggest item at 1 and at 5.
- **Good:** At 1 it feels like the game you already know. At 5 it is a little better, not a big jump.
- **Checks:** Employee stats change play

### S12. Mr. Harlow

- **Do:** Vacuum in a room next to him. Then let him chase you through doorways and round desks. Lose him and duck into a side room behind his back.
- **Good:** Vacuuming gets a "hm?" and a mild look, no chase music. He does not snag or swing wide into door frames. He does not spot you through the doorway unless he is right there.
- **Checks:** He no longer snags on door frames; No chases through walls behind him; Noise makes him look, not chase

### S13. Finish the shift

- **Do:** Make the quota, bank, and clock out.
- **Good:** The goal-met jingle, results music and chase music end cleanly, not cut off. The results card makes sense.
- **Checks:** Music ends cleanly; Rank lanyard and badge

### S14. Emotes and steps

- **Do:** Play Bubble Ride, Big Roundup and Cocoa Sip. Walk and jump as Cappy and as Prickles.
- **Good:** Sounds land on the action. The lasso has the coil's colours. Cocoa Sip shows only the mug. Cappy's steps have no plastic rattle.
- **Checks:** Character sounds; Legendary emotes

## Session 2: with a 2nd client

### D1. Host a crew

- **Do:** Studio: Test > Clients and Servers, 2 players. Player 1 steps onto a bay and hosts. Player 2 joins, then tries LEAVE, then joins again. Press START.
- **Good:** Player 2 sees the same truck pop in. LEAVE works. Both go into the shift together.
- **Checks:** Truck v3 Moving Van, bigger bed; Crew hosting in the hub

### D2. What the other player sees

- **Do:** Player 1 wears a Legendary skin, plays an emote and throws a sticker. Then switch to Cappy and walk and crouch.
- **Good:** Player 2 sees the same idle prop, emote and sticker in the same spot. Cappy's shorts bend with his legs.
- **Checks:** Legendary skins have their own idles; The three Legendary skins; Legendary emotes; Gloom-Touched skins; Cappy's shorts follow his legs; Chat wheel, pings and stickers; Sticker toss

### D3. Inside vs outside sound

- **Do:** Player 1 goes inside. Player 2 stays in the yard.
- **Good:** Player 2 hears only the yard. Player 1 hears only the office.
- **Checks:** Inside sounds are silent outside

### D4. Abilities together

- **Do:** Pippa uses Let's Go! next to the other player. Cappy uses Spot It!. Prickles uses Potted Plant while Harlow is near.
- **Good:** Both players get the speed boost. The other player sees the outline or the plant.
- **Checks:** Abilities: Let's Go!, Spot It!, Potted Plant

### D5. Clean together

- **Do:** Both vacuum the same pile. Both spam E on the same prompt.
- **Good:** The pile goes faster with two. Nothing breaks or doubles.
- **Checks:** Employee stats change play; Rate limit on prompts
