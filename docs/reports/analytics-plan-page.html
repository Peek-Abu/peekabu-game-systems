What should the game count?

You asked if we can see where players leave and what they buy. Your picks are in and built. Below: the off switches, where to find each number, and how to read them.

What we count today

Roblox counts for free
  • Who comes back the next day, week and month
  • How long a visit lasts
  • Robux spent on each pack, Coffee, deal and the Big Crew pass
We already count
  • A new player's first steps: hub, truck, shift, clean, bank, results
  • Shift events: catches, knock-outs, wipes, how a shift ended
  • Credits and Gloom from shift pay
Missing
  • Anything bought with Gleam, Credits, Gloom or acorns
  • Where and when players quit
  • Time in the hub
  • Training steps
  • Shifts per visit

The big one: every shop, Locker, Coffee, hire and bundle buy is counted nowhere today. Robux buys still show up, because Roblox counts those by itself.

The picks

1

Where players quit, and right after what

When a player quits the game (not when they ride a truck), we note where they were (hub, training, shift or results screen) and what just happened: caught in the last minute, being chased, knocked out, or nothing.

Tells you things like: "30% of quits in a shift come right after a catch." Knock-outs are kept apart, so deaths don't mix in.

2

Time in the hub

Each hub visit, we note how many seconds the player stayed, whether they got on a truck or quit, and whether it was their first visit.

Tells you things like: "New players stay 4 minutes and 1 in 5 quit before a truck. Make the hub faster."

3

Training, step by step

A list of steps: got on the training truck, then each of the 5 stops (goo, treasure, quiet hall, hide, done), then back in the hub. Players who skip training are counted too.

Tells you things like: "40% quit at the hide stop." Compare skippers and finishers: do skippers come back less?

4

Shifts per visit

When a player quits, we note how many shifts they played that visit, and how many in total so far.

Tells you things like: "Players play 2.3 shifts and then quit. Most stop after shift 2."

5

Every buy and every reward

Every time Gleam, Credits, Gloom or acorns go in or out, we note the exact item and its rarity. That covers shop items, bundles, Coffee, Lockers (including pity and copies), hires, School, deals, packs and rewards.

Tells you things like: "Bubble Buddy sells 3 times more than any other Epic." "Most Gleam goes to Lockers, not the shop." "Players run out of Credits by shift 5."

6

Look, then buy

Each shop visit: opened the shop, looked at an item, bought it. We also note which item they looked at.

Tells you things like: "Lots of players look at this skin, but few buy it. The price may be too high."

7

Fix what we count today

Three small fixes. The training truck is counted as a "first real truck" right now, so it gets its own count. The game's version number is dropped from event details, because otherwise Roblox's detail limit fills up after about 25 updates. Banking sends one event per load instead of one per item, to save the event budget.

Without this, the new-player numbers come out wrong, and after a few weeks the details turn into "Other".

Built (17 Nov)

You said yes to picks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7, and no to 6 (the shop funnel). All of them are built and sent to the game branch. They start counting once the game is published.

An off switch for each pick

Picks 1 to 5 each have their own on/off switch. When a switch is off, that tracking stops completely. Nothing for it runs and nothing is sent. Pick 7's fixes stay on all the time.

Way 1, no update needed: Creator Hub → your game → Configs. Make a true/false config with the switch's name and publish it as false. Live servers pick it up in about a minute. Set it back to true (or delete it) to turn it on again.

Way 2, in the code: the file Features/Analytics/Data/AnalyticsSwitchConstants.luau has one true/false line per pick. The game uses it when Way 1 has no value. Changing it takes an update.

Where to find it

Creator Hub → Creations → Odd Hours → Analytics. Then:

  • Custom Events: left_game, hub_visit, visit_ended, training_skipped. Split them by "Custom Field 1, 2 or 3" to see the details below.
  • Funnels: first_time (a new player's first shift) and training (the training stops).
  • Economy: Sources (money in) and Sinks (money out) for Gleam, Credits, Gloom and Acorns. Split by Item SKU, then by the custom fields for the exact item.
  • Retention, Engagement and Monetization: free from Roblox. Who comes back, how long visits last, and Robux sales.

Only the live game sends data. Studio sends nothing. Numbers show up about a day later, and Roblox keeps them for 90 days.

How to read the numbers after launch

Wait about a week before you decide anything, so one odd day doesn't fool you. Then compare each week to the week before. These are starting guesses, not rules. Your first week's numbers become your normal.
